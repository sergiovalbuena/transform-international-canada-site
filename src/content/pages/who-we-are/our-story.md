    heading: Three reasons we *came together*
    lede: false
    body:
      - First, after decades in international development, we have seen the power of collaboration—and the cost when it is absent. Too often when organizations work in isolation, valuable knowledge remains siloed, lessons go unshared, mistakes are repeated, and effective solutions are overlooked. When organizations openly share knowledge and experience, everyone benefits, and development outcomes improve.
      - Second, we believe that when people are equipped to help themselves, they fully embrace responsibility for their programs and future. Lasting development takes time, and solutions designed and led locally are far more likely to endure.
      - Third, we realized that, together, our networks, expertise and influence form a powerful international resource. We felt compelled to put it to work.
