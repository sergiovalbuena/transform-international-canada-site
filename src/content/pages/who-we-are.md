      - Transform International (Canada) (TIC) – and its sister organization in the United States, Transform International (TI) – were founded by seasoned, passionate development professionals united by a shared commitment to improving the lives of individuals and communities in need. We believe strongly in the power of collaboration and do our best to provide practical, useful information when working in the developing world. By drawing on their experience, knowledge and insights, we can direct our support where it will have the greatest impact, helping locally led programs succeed and endure.
      - We are lean, mostly volunteer-driven organizations committed to servant leadership. We believe lasting change begins with local leadership, so we work alongside in-country partners who understand their communities best and are invested in their long-term success. Together, we develop sustainable solutions using approaches and technologies that are socially, culturally, and geographically appropriate.
