    name: Joyce
    role: Community Health Club graduate, Rumphi District
    story:
      - Joyce, a single mother of three, never knew how diseases spread or how simple hygiene and sanitation practices could protect her family and her community. Through her local Community Health Club in Rumphi District, she learned about handwashing, sanitation, disease prevention, and other issues—including gender-based violence. Led by trained local facilitators, the weekly sessions equipped her with practical skills that proved invaluable during a cholera outbreak in 2025.
      - After graduating, Joyce and her fellow participants asked for business training. TIC arranged for two local community development consultants to be trained in the Street Business School methodology and helped the Health Clubs establish savings accounts where members contribute weekly and access small loans to start businesses. As health improves and incomes grow, neighbouring villagers are eager to participate, creating a ripple effect of lasting change.
