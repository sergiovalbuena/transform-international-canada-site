**2020 — A new kind of school.** Transform International helped THARS launch entrepreneurial
training using the social-franchise model of Street Business School, an organization based in
Kampala, Uganda. Street Business School teaches practical skills, from market research to
bookkeeping, and coaches graduates as they set out.

**2021 — Training the trainers.** We secured funding for THARS to hire two economic advisers and
send them to Street Business School. Both graduated in August 2021, came home to Gitega and
translated the course from English into Kirundi.

**A bread oven that teaches.** At the THARS centre in Gitega we helped build an energy-efficient
commercial bread oven. It saves THARS money on the meals it serves at workshops, uses less
firewood, and shows participants how a baking business can work.

**2024 — Diane’s story.** Diane Nzeyimana, 28, from Mukoro hill, used to mend clothes for
neighbours. Through Street Business School she built a sewing business that now designs new
clothing too. As her income grew, she bought a cow and several pigs to earn more — and she asked
us to pass on a big thank-you to everyone who made it possible.

**January 2026 — 72 graduates.** Cohorts 7, 8 and 9 graduated together in Gitega. Every one of the
72 participants completed the training and launched their own income-generating activity.

**March 2026 — Building momentum.** After interviewing more than 170 candidates, THARS recruited
64 new participants. Cohorts 10 and 11 are underway, and everyone has completed baseline coaching.
Cohorts now have up to 33 participants, and THARS is on track to graduate four full cohorts in 2026
for the first time. The two Burundian instructors, both certified by Street Business School, teach
the program entirely in Kirundi. Next goal: a second Street Business School in Muyinga.
