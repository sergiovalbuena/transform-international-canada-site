  building-stronger-health:
    type: text
    eyebrow: STREAMS
    heading: Building stronger health systems *from the ground up*
    body:
      - Across Africa, many healthcare facilities continue to operate without reliable access to clean water, sanitation, and hygiene. These critical gaps undermine patient safety, compromise dignity, and place additional strain on healthcare workers—most of whom are women.
      - STREAMS changes that reality. By bringing sustainable WASH systems to healthcare facilities, the program is helping build stronger health systems from the ground up. The program creates cleaner, safer environments for patients, empowers frontline healthcare workers with the tools and training they need, and lays the foundation for better health outcomes for generations to come.
    callouts:
      - text: WASH is a set of globally recognized solutions addressing three essential elements of public health — safe and reliable water, sanitation and hygiene practices.

  about:
    type: text
    id: about
    eyebrow: About STREAMS
    heading: Systems, Training, Empowerment, and *Monitoring Support*
    body:
      - The STREAMS (Systems, Training, Empowerment, and Monitoring Support) approach was developed through an international partnership led by Transform International (United States), the Desert Research Institute (United States), and Development Action for Marginalized Rural Areas (DAMRA) (Malawi) in collaboration with Transform International (Canada) and Royal Roads University (Canada).
      - STREAMS was designed as a sustainability strategy to strengthen healthcare infrastructure and improve quality of care in low-resource settings, in Rumphi District, Malawi.

  circuit-riders:
    type: text
    id: circuit-riders
    eyebrow: Circuit Riders
    heading: Two types of *roving experts*
    body:
      - Two types of roving experts provide consistent, on-the-ground support.
    list:
      - "**Technical Circuit Riders** are trained technicians who travel regularly to healthcare facilities to inspect and maintain WASH infrastructure, repair equipment, test water quality, and train staff in preventive maintenance."
      - "**Quality-of-care Circuit Riders** are health sector professionals who coach healthcare workers in hand hygiene, infection prevention, environmental cleaning, waste management, and gender-responsive practices, and assist managers in strengthening systems and monitoring accountability."
    callouts:
      - text: A lack of basic WASH services increases hospital-acquired infections, puts mothers and newborns at risk, and undermines public trust in healthcare.

  malawi:
    type: text
    id: malawi
    eyebrow: Rumphi District
    heading: STREAMS in *Malawi*
    body:
      - Rumphi District, located in northern Malawi, hosts 20 health care centres including two hospitals. Before TIC involvement, most facilities had broken or rudimentary sanitary systems and no reliable, clean water.
      - Since 2017, TIC has worked with our partners, regional health authorities, and local entrepreneurs to implement STREAMS. We also work with funding partners to source and coordinate resources for major initiatives such as recent major upgrades to water systems and installation of water purification systems.
    quote:
      text: As a pregnant woman about to deliver my baby, I had to walk a kilometre to fetch water for the labour ward as there was no water supply at the hospital. Now, thanks to STREAMS, we have lots of water, convenient places to wash hands, clean toilets, and more. We are so happy!
      attribution: A mother in Rumphi District
    callouts:
      - text: Providing these essential WASH services protects public health, upholds the human right to water and sanitation, reduces the burden of collecting water—particularly for women and girls—and improves health, education, and quality of care in healthcare facilities.

  outcomes:
    type: text
    id: outcomes
    eyebrow: Recent outcomes
    heading: Key infrastructure improvements in *Rumphi District*
    body:
      - This year, we continued implementing, with our partners, the Circuit Rider program in Rumphi to sustain water, sanitation, and hygiene infrastructure and behaviours in healthcare facilities (HCFs).
      - We worked with the Rotary Club of West Shore, and the Rotary WASH E-club to provide key infrastructure improvements in Rumphi District.
    list:
      - Installation of water purification systems at David Gordon Memorial Hospital
      - Installation of six new 5,000-litre water storage tanks
      - Construction of a new water tank stand at Mphompa HCF
      - Upgrades to the water system at Mhuju HCF

  likoma:
    type: text
    id: likoma
    eyebrow: Recent outcomes
    heading: Expanding into *Likoma District*
    body:
      - We expanded into Likoma District (islands in Lake Malawi) in partnership with the District Environmental Health Officer, a former Quality of Care Circuit Rider from Rumphi District.
    list:
      - Launching STREAMS in the district's three healthcare facilities
      - Delivering WASH Fit (WHO's Facility Improvement Tool) training for staff
      - Developing Chichewa-language educational posters promoting handwashing, sanitation, and hygiene

  looking-ahead:
    type: text
    id: looking-ahead
    eyebrow: Looking ahead
    heading: Every health facility up to *minimum service standards*
    body:
      - As we strengthen the sustainability of WASH systems, our goal is to bring every health facility up to minimum service standards. Working in close collaboration with government leaders, STREAMS is continuing in Rumphi District and developing as a program in Likoma District – to build stronger health systems and ensure lasting, locally led improvements.
      - "In the planning stages for the near future, we are:"
    list:
      - Seeking funding to address urgent sanitation needs at St. Peter's Hospital in Likoma District as existing septic facilities have reached capacity
      - Exploring the use of small, solar-powered electric vehicles to safely transport medical waste to Likoma's Chima Hospital, which has a modern World Bank-funded incinerator
      - Partnering with Rotary Clubs to raise funds for needed upgrades to healthcare facilities in Rumphi and Likoma districts
      - Finalizing a revised STREAMS monitoring system to provide clearer performance metrics that can drive evidence-based decision-making
